# 🎯 Creating Segments in Marketing Cloud Next (MCN)

After building Unified Customer Profiles in Data Cloud, the next step is identifying the right audience. 

> 👉 **This is where Segments come in.**

A segment is a group of customers who share specific attributes, behaviors, or business criteria. Instead of sending the same message to everyone, marketers can target the audience that matters most.

---

### 📂 Examples of Segments

*   ✅ **Newsletter Subscribers**
*   ✅ **Customers who made a purchase** in the last 30 days
*   ✅ **Customers who have not engaged** with emails in the last 90 days
*   ✅ **High-Value Customers**

---

### 🛠️ Real-World Example

During an **IP Warming implementation**, we created audience segments based on engagement and sending strategy. This allowed us to gradually activate specific groups of subscribers while monitoring performance and deliverability.

---

### ❓ Why Segments Matter

| Without Segmentation (❌) | With Segmentation (✅) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Generic messaging | Relevant communication |
| Lower engagement | Better targeting |
| Poor customer experience | Improved engagement & personalized experiences |

---

### 💡 Key Takeaway

> **Data Cloud helps you understand your customers. Segments help you identify which customers to engage.**
> 
> Effective segmentation is the foundation of successful marketing campaigns.
