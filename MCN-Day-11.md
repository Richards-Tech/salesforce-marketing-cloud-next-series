# 🧩 Identity Resolution in Salesforce Data Cloud

Imagine a customer interacts with your business through multiple channels:
*   📧 Uses one email address to subscribe to a newsletter.
*   🛒 Makes a purchase using another email address.
*   📱 Provides a phone number through a web form.

To a system, these may appear as separate records. But in reality, **they belong to the same person.**

This is where **Identity Resolution** comes in. It helps Data Cloud identify and connect records that belong to the same customer, creating a more complete customer view.

---

### ❓ Why is it Important?

| Without Identity Resolution (❌) | With Identity Resolution (✅) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Duplicate customer records | Unified customer profiles |
| Fragmented customer data | Better audience targeting |
| Inaccurate segmentation | More accurate customer insights |
| Poor personalization | Improved personalization |

---

### 💡 Simple Example

*   **Record 1:** 📧 `john.doe@gmail.com` | 📱 `XXX-XXX-1234`
*   **Record 2:** 📧 `j.doe@gmail.com` | 📱 `XXX-XXX-1234`

Although the email addresses are different, Data Cloud can use **matching rules** and identifiers (like the phone number) to determine that these records belong to the same customer.

> 👉 **The result:** One unified customer profile instead of multiple disconnected records.

---

### 💡 Key Takeaway

> **Identity Resolution is what transforms customer records into customer profiles.**
> 
> It's one of the key capabilities that enables Customer 360 and personalized engagement in Data Cloud.
