# 🔄 Understanding Segment Refresh in Data Cloud

Creating a segment is only the first step. But what happens when customer data changes?

> 👉 **That's where Segment Refresh comes in.**

A segment is not a static list. Customers can enter or leave a segment as their attributes, behaviors, or activities change.

---

### 🛠️ Real-World Example

A business creates a segment for:
> 🛒 **Customers Who Purchased in the Last 90 Days**

*   **Every day:** New customers make purchases and qualify for the segment.
*   **At the same time:** Customers whose last purchase was more than 90 days ago automatically drop out of the audience.

**Segment Refresh** ensures the audience always reflects the latest customer behavior without requiring manual updates.

---

### ❓ Why is Segment Refresh Important?

| Without Refresh (❌) | With Refresh (✅) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Audiences become outdated | Up-to-date audiences |
| Inaccurate targeting | Accurate targeting |
| Irrelevant customer communications | Better customer experiences & effective activations |

---

### ⚙️ How It Works

```text
Customer Data Changes ➔ Segment Criteria Evaluated ➔ Segment Updated ➔ Activation Uses Latest Audience

```
💡 Key Takeaway
A segment is not a one-time audience list.

Segment Refresh ensures your audience stays aligned with the latest customer data, making activations and campaigns more accurate and effective.
